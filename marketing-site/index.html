Fabrics and colours

Every fabric, without extra photography

A sofa in 25 fabrics needs one good photo, not 25. Visully reads the colours you already have in Shopify and fills the gaps for you, so shoppers can switch fabric in the preview with one tap.

1

Shopify swatches. Colour or image swatches on your variants are used as they are.

2

Your own colours. Pick a colour per fabric in the Fabrics & Colours manager, or upload a sample.

3

Filled automatically. With auto-fill on, the colour is read from the variant photo, then from the name. One-colour products simply use their photos.

For merchants

Built to be switched on and left alone

Everything lives inside your Shopify admin. Nothing to host, no photos stored, and a bill you can predict.

Credits, not surprises

Each preview uses one credit. Your plan refreshes monthly, and usage stops at your allowance. Buy a top-up only when you want to. A usage page shows credits by day and by product.

Shopper limits you set

Each shopper can make 10 previews a day by default. Change it per store. Unusual bursts are paused automatically and flagged on your home page.

Emails into Shopify

Ask for an email before or after the first image, required or optional, or not at all. Addresses go straight into your Shopify customers with marketing consent recorded, ready for your email tool.

Sales from previews

Previews are linked to the basket, so orders that follow a preview are counted. The report appears on your home page once the numbers are meaningful.

Privacy by design

Room photos and generated images are never stored on our servers. Shopify’s customer data requests and deletions are handled for you.

Getting started

Live on your store this afternoon

STEP 1

Install from the Shopify App Store

Approve the app and choose a plan, or stay on Free. Visully reads your products and nothing else.

STEP 2

Pick the products

Switch on the sofas, beds and rugs you want. For each one, confirm the reference photo and check the fabric colours it found.

STEP 3

Turn on the embed

Enable Visully in your theme’s app embeds, choose a style and where the button sits. The Room Assistant is a second switch.

Do I need 3D models of my products?

No. Visully works from your product photos. A clear photo of the product on a plain background gives the best results.

How accurate are the previews?

They are AI images for illustration, and we say so under every preview. Scale, colour and details can differ, so shoppers are reminded to check dimensions before ordering.

What happens to shoppers’ photos?

They are used for the preview and discarded. We do not keep room photos or generated images, and the shopper downloads their result from the popup.

Can a shopper run up my bill?

No. Usage stops at your plan’s allowance, each shopper has a daily limit you control, and sudden bursts are paused automatically.

Does it work on themes other than Dawn?

Yes. It is a theme app extension, so it works with any Online Store 2.0 theme. The button position can be adjusted per theme.
